The original Andrew the Spire score and victory cadence use recorded bowed-string samples from VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios, recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell. Sample cutting: Elan Hickler / Soundemote.

Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
License: CC0 1.0, included as VSCO-LICENSE.txt.

Every musical voice is a recorded violin, violin section, viola, short-bowed viola, or cello. There are no piano, bell, synthesizer or percussion instruments in these two music files. Combat sound effects are separate.

Rebuild: run scripts/fetch-strings.py, then scripts/render-score.py and scripts/render-victory.py with Python and NumPy. The source recordings are cached in scripts/string-samples (not bundled into the app).

Current electric arrangement: score-electric.wav and victory-electric.wav, generated by scripts/render-electric.py. Four amplified bowed parts (two violins, viola, cello) use the same CC0 recordings. Lead guitar uses the local TimGM6mb overdriven guitar preset rendered through FluidSynth. Soundfont author/license information is included in ELECTRIC-GUITAR-CREDITS.txt; the soundfont itself is not bundled. Rebuilding also requires libfluidsynth.so.3 and /usr/share/sounds/sf2/TimGM6mb.sf2.

Current room arrangements: room-title.wav, room-map.wav, room-crossing.wav, room-reward.wav, room-well.wav, room-boss.wav and room-victory.wav, generated by scripts/render-rooms.py. These original arrangements use the same CC0 VSCO recordings for four bowed parts and TimGM6mb clean electric guitar (zero-based preset 27) through FluidSynth. The soundfont credits above still apply. Rebuild after fetching string samples, with Python, NumPy, libfluidsynth.so.3 and the local TimGM6mb soundfont.
